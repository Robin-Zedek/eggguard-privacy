2. Advertising and consent

The game shows rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. Ads are optional - you watch them only if you choose to unlock a reward. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and other regions where it is required, we ask for your consent (using Google's User Messaging Platform) before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad Privacy (shown where required). More information: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

3. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore) - firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase - firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google AdMob - policies.google.com/privacy

These providers process data on our behalf or as independent controllers under their own policies. We do not sell your personal data.

4. Retention and deleting your EggGuard account

How to delete your account:

  • In the game: Settings → Delete Account → confirm.
  • By email: send a request to info@pink-future.cz with your player name. We will delete your account within 30 days.

What is deleted: your anonymous account ID, player name, leaderboard scores, friend code, friends, duel invitations and match data, and all progress stored on your device (coins, skins, levels, achievements, settings).

What may be kept: reports you submitted about other players' names may be kept for up to 90 days for moderation. Usage data in Google Analytics for Firebase is not linked to your player name and is automatically deleted after 14 months. Advertising data is handled by Google AdMob under its own policy.

Uninstalling the game removes the data stored on your device.

5. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child has provided data, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU/EEA under the GDPR) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Use the in-game options above or contact us.

7. Security

Data is transmitted to Firebase over encrypted connections. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
